Our story

A neighborhood table with a little spark.

Ember & Olive began with a simple idea: create the kind of place where a great meal feels relaxed, personal, and worth remembering.

Our kitchen celebrates seasonal ingredients, open-fire cooking, and the generous flavors we love to share with friends. Our dining room is warm, unfussy, and made for long conversations.
