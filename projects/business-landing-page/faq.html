Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Do you take reservations?

Yes. Reservations are recommended for dinner and can be requested through our contact page or by phone.

Do you offer vegetarian options?

Yes. Our menu always includes vegetarian options, and our team is happy to explain ingredients or adapt dishes where possible.

Can you accommodate allergies?

Please tell your server about any allergies when booking and again when you arrive. We will do our best to guide you safely.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday through Sunday from 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM. Hours may change for holidays.

Do you host private events?

Yes. Contact us with your preferred date, guest count, and event style so we can discuss options.
